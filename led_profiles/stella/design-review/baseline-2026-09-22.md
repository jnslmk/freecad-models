## Baseline fit and design observations

- All four saved documents opened through FreeCAD MCP. The inspected final
  bodies and representative links have valid one-solid shapes, and no document
  object reports `Error` state. This is a pre-edit baseline, not post-change
  verification.
- **Functional issue:** each of the 24 arm/core pairs has the same minimum
  separation, **5.084843 mm**, with zero material overlap. The current core
  parameter `StellaParams.CoreHeight` is **12 mm**, while the assembly layout
  script places arm roots at a hard-coded 20 mm core-axis offset. That mismatch
  is a likely cause, but the intended datum/stack must be confirmed before a
  correction. The assembly's overlap-only validation does not detect this gap.
- The current arm has **0.035 mm** minimum distance to the aluminium profile
  envelope; the clamp has **0.083926 mm** to its local profile reference. The
  arm and clamp touch with zero volumetric intersection in their common local
  coordinates. These nominal CAD distances do not prove printable/physical fit.
- The saved assembly explicitly reports `CableRouteClear = False` and
  `MaxCableArmOverlap = 316.417 mm³` for the axial cable-stub route. Its own
  note says an arm-side cabling-hole decision remains open.
- At each core, three large rounded arm shoulders visually crowd the organic
  three-lobed core; the 5 mm separation currently makes the interface read as
  stacked, disconnected plates rather than a resolved joint. The arm's broad
  shell/saddle transition is much softer than the clamp's crisp extruded arch
  and square boss edges. The two keeper lands and their tapers add another
  surface rhythm at the profile-to-arm transition. The core uses a 0.6 mm
  perimeter chamfer, while the arm uses an R2 connector rim and the clamp has
  no final edge treatment feature.

## Proposed order — approval required before geometry edits

1. **Resolve the functional baseline:** decide whether the 12 mm core height is
   locked and the arm placements must be reconciled, or the core/seat stack is
   supposed to be 20 mm. Keep the key, pilots, screw axes, and profile endpoints
   fixed unless separately approved. Establish positive seating/contact and
   non-interference in a working copy before any styling work.
2. **Define the visible core–arm seam:** test one reversible native shoulder/
   perimeter treatment that makes the three arm roots subordinate to the core
   without altering the 28 mm key or mounting holes. Compare the local and
   full assembly views against this baseline.
3. **Coordinate arm–clamp and saddle edges:** test a modest, shared exposed-edge
   hierarchy and a cleaner transition at the keeper lands. Do not change the
   profile cavity, screw/counterbore envelopes, cable route, or bearing faces
   for appearance alone. Select actual radii/reveals only after viewing the
   first concept and checking minimum wall/clearance.

Separate decisions remain on whether all arms require installed clamps in the
assembly and how the flagged cable/arm overlap should be routed. No aesthetic
feature should conceal those unresolved functional interfaces.
