The existing full assembly generator now reads `StellaCore.StellaParams.CoreHeight`
instead of assuming a 20 mm arm-root offset, links `StellaProfileClamp.ProfileClampBody`
at each of the 24 arm placements, records `CoreSeatHeight` and `ClampInstances`

`STELLA_OUTPUT_PATH` allows a new output copy while still refusing to replace an
existing file. The generator does not edit the source component documents.

## Measured result and verification

- The old saved assembly has **5.084843 mm** minimum separation at all 24
  arm–core pairs. The 12 mm working assembly has **0 mm separation and 0 mm³
  material overlap** at all 24 after recompute and after close/reload.
- The working assembly has 8 linked cores, 24 linked arms, **24 linked clamps**,
  and 96 links for the 12 lamp assemblies. All inspected links are valid; no
  object reports `Error` after reload. Clamp–arm, clamp–aluminium, and
  clamp–diffuser volumetric overlap are zero at all 24 stations. The minimum
  clamp–aluminium clearance is **0.083926 mm**; arm–aluminium clearance at a
  checked station is **0.035 mm**. These are CAD nominal values, not physical
  fit allowances.
- The 1500 mm profile length and endpoint line/span checks remain fixed. To
  reconcile that length with 12 mm core seating and unchanged arm geometry,
  the **base tetrahedron side changes from 1706.394890 to 1693.330945 mm** and
  the **offset side from 1677.403512 to 1664.339567 mm** (both 13.063945 mm
  shorter). This overall-envelope change is a **consequential choice requiring
  acceptance**, not an approved replacement of the original assembly.
- Concept A's clamp Body tip is `ExposedBossEdgeBlend`, a single native fillet
  after `CounterboredM3Clearance`. It removes **11.5885 mm³** only from the
  original clamp, with no added material and unchanged bounding extents. Its
  R1.5 → R1.0 mm parameter test changed the volume from **3430.9451 to
  3437.3832 mm³**; R1.5 restored the original concept volume without rerunning
  a script. The body, expression, link source, and representative mating
  clearances survived save/close/reload.

## Unresolved functional and design decisions

- The original and both working layouts still have **six aluminium–aluminium
  crossings**, each with about **967.3 mm³** of intersection. This predates the
  12 mm correction; the maximum changed by less than 0.001 mm³. The saved
  `CrossingOffset = 30.75 mm` is not a verified physical crossing clearance.
  A frame separation/orientation change is a product-level tradeoff and was
  not made in this review.
- The existing axial cable stubs still intersect their arms by up to
  **316.417 mm³**; `CableRouteClear` remains `False`. No cable geometry was
  changed pending routing/strain-relief and electrical decisions.
- Concept A is deliberately restrained and does not resolve the broad,
  overlapping arm-shoulder silhouette at the core. More aggressive seams or
  smaller shoulder mass could alter the 28 × 28 × 3 mm key support, M3 access,
  wall thickness, or the overall visual hierarchy, so those were not guessed.
- The generated assembly uses native, editable `App::Link` placements, but the
  saved assembly's `CoreSeatHeight` property records the generation input; it
  does **not** itself drive all link placements by expression. Regenerate a new
  copy with the single full assembly generator if the core height/layout
  changes. Component sketches/features remain natively parametric.
- Physical fit, print tolerances, fastener installation, suspended-use strength,
  electrical routing, and diffuser/gland assembly still require human checks.

No copy is declared the accepted final design; the authoritative originals
remain intact until the frame-envelope, crossing, cable, and visual decisions
are made.
